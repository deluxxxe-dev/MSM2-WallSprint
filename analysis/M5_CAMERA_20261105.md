## M5c (one change): camera-relative stick
Ground calibration with a sideways stick: +0x1E0 must be perpendicular to travel and travel = sign(sx)*h*cross(forward, up); this both
rejects a hero-forward copy and yields handedness h (`WS_CAMERA_HANDEDNESS`). After acceptance, WS maps the stick as
d = sx*h*cross(p, n) + sy*p, p = camera forward projected on the wall (normal n); otherwise the M3d mapping stays.
Camera close-approach on upward WS is deferred (next camera task).
