# M5 camera — step 1 config decode (2026-11-05)

Owner-extracted vanilla `configs/camera/camera_wallrun_aimcontext.config` (532 B) and `configs/camera/bmath_sandman_wallrun.config` (520 B),
decoded with source/tools/dat1_config_fields.py (VERIFIED-STATIC):
- type `CameraAimContextConfig`; fields FieldOfView, OffsetDown{X,Y,Z}, OffsetLevel{X,Y,Z}, OffsetUp{X,Y,Z}, PivotHeight.
- wallrun aim context offsets e.g. (1.25, 0, -2.25) and (0.5, 0, -3.25); sandman variant small/zero offsets.
Conclusion: these configs only move the camera pivot/offset and FOV. There is no up-vector/roll/orientation field, so the
"wall reads as floor" camera roll cannot come from these assets (offset/FOV tuning remains possible later).

## M5c (one change): camera-relative stick
Ground calibration with a sideways stick: +0x1E0 must be perpendicular to travel and travel = sign(sx)*h*cross(forward, up); this both
rejects a hero-forward copy and yields handedness h (`WS_CAMERA_HANDEDNESS`). After acceptance, WS maps the stick as
d = sx*h*cross(p, n) + sy*p, p = camera forward projected on the wall (normal n); otherwise the M3d mapping stays.
Camera close-approach on upward WS is deferred (next camera task).
